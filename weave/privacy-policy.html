Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026

Weave is provided by SangsangPlus. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Weave mobile application.

In short: Weave does not collect your personal information. It has no user accounts, no servers, no advertising, and no analytics. Everything you create in Weave stays on your device.

1. Information You Enter in the App

Weave lets you create Modes and Routines, each with a name, a start time, an icon, and related settings. It also collects short answers during the first-run questions, such as when your day begins and ends and what you would like to include in it.

This information is stored on your device only. SangsangPlus does not operate a user account system for Weave, does not provide a server-based sync service, and does not receive a copy of what you enter.

2. Information Collected Automatically

Weave does not collect analytics, usage statistics, crash reports, advertising identifiers, device identifiers, or location information. The app does not send data to SangsangPlus servers, because SangsangPlus does not operate a server for Weave.

3. On-Device Generation

On supported devices, Weave can draft a suggested day from your answers to the first-run questions. This drafting is performed by Apple Intelligence on your device.

Your answers are processed locally on your device for this purpose. They are not transmitted to SangsangPlus, and SangsangPlus does not receive the generated result. Where the feature is unavailable, Weave produces the same kind of draft using rules built into the app, again entirely on your device.

4. Advertising

Weave does not display advertisements and does not include advertising or tracking software development kits.

5. Device Permissions

Weave asks for permission to schedule alarms and notifications so that a routine can ring at its start time. The permission is requested through the operating system, is used only to present your own routines on your own device, and can be reviewed or changed at any time in your device settings. Declining it does not prevent you from using the rest of the app.

6. Data Sharing

SangsangPlus does not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties. Because the information you enter remains on your device, there is no user content for SangsangPlus to share.

If you download Weave from an app store, the store operator handles the distribution and any payment according to its own privacy policy. SangsangPlus does not receive your payment card information.

7. Children and Younger Users

Weave is a general audience app and is not restricted to adults. Planning a school holiday or a study period is one of the things it is made for.

Weave does not collect personal information from any user, of any age. There is no sign-up, no user account, and no transmission of what you enter, so nothing is gathered from a younger user that could be gathered differently from an adult. Weave also contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking software.

Because nothing reaches SangsangPlus, there is no record of a child's use of Weave for us to review or delete. Routines created on a device can be removed in the app or by uninstalling it.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Your Modes and Routines remain on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app. Uninstalling Weave removes the data the app has stored on your device. Because SangsangPlus holds no copy, there is nothing further for you to request that we delete.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the app changes. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised.
