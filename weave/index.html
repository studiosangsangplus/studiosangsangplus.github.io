Weave

One ends, the next begins.

Weave is a daily routine planner for people without a fixed schedule. Build a day of small habits that follow one another, and let each one call you when its time comes.

A day you can actually keep

Answer a few questions

Tell Weave when your day starts and ends and what you want in it. It drafts a whole day for you to edit, instead of handing you a blank page.

Habits that link together

Chain one routine to the next, so finishing the first is what starts the second. Small habits are easier to keep when they hold on to each other.

Modes for different days

A weekday is not a Saturday. Keep separate sets of routines and let the right one apply on the right days.

Alarms that reach you

Each routine rings on your lock screen at its start time, with sound, even when the screen is off.

Private by design

Weave has no accounts, no servers, and no advertising. Your modes and routines stay on your device. The draft that Weave writes from your answers is generated on your device using Apple Intelligence — your answers are not sent to SangsangPlus or anyone else.

Our approach

Weave is built around one idea from the childhood daily-schedule wheel: a day is easier to follow when it is drawn as a whole, not kept as a list of separate reminders. Everything else is kept out of the way.
